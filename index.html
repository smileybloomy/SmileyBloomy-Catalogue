⚠️ Important Notice - Patch Test Recommended

As our soaps are handmade with natural ingredients, we recommend performing a patch test on a small area of skin before full use. Apply a small amount to your inner forearm, wait 24 hours, and check for any reaction. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. For external use only. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
